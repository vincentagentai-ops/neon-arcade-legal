Privacy Policy

“Neon Arcade” app (Stack Tower, Neon Flap & Neon Switch) — last updated: October 5, 2026

In one sentence: Neon Arcade is free and requires no account (Google Play Games is optional). It shows ads provided by Google AdMob: optional videos (to “Continue” after losing a game or add 15 seconds to the clock, “Double the shards” you earned, replace a daily mission, keep a day streak after missing a day, or try a cosmetic for 24 hours — each one has an ad-free equivalent), and a full-screen ad from time to time between two games (about every 5 to 7 games). The developer collects no data: what the app saves — your scores, statistics, progress and settings — stays on your device (and, if you have turned it on, in your account’s Android backup) and is never sent to the developer. An optional streak reminder notification is offered, turned off by default: it is scheduled on your device and sends no data.

1. Who provides this app

Neon Arcade is developed and distributed independently. For any question about this privacy policy or the app, you can contact the developer at the address shown on its Google Play listing.

2. No data is collected by the developer

The developer of Neon Arcade runs no user accounts, no servers and no usage analytics SDK. The app itself sends no information about you to any server belonging to the developer — there is none.

The app does request Internet access, solely for the ads described below (section 3) and, if you turn them on, for Google Play Games services (section 3a). The game itself needs no network connection.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows two kinds of ads:

  • Optional rewarded video ads: a button marked with an “AD” badge lets you watch a video. On the game over screen, to “Continue” the game where it stopped (in Time Attack mode, to add 15 seconds to the clock, once per game) or to “Double the shards” earned during the game (in-game currency with no real value). In the menu, to replace a daily mission (once a day) or to keep your day streak after missing a single day (offered at most once a week). In the collection, to try a cosmetic for 24 hours. Only one ad button is offered at a time, and the video only plays if you tap it. Everything a video gives you can also be obtained by playing or with shards.
  • A full-screen ad between two games: about every 5 to 7 games (all games combined), an ad may be shown just before a new game starts. If no ad is available (for example when offline), the game starts normally.

The game remains fully playable without ever watching the optional ads.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is loaded or shown, Google (as a data controller separate from the developer) may collect and process certain technical data through its SDK, including:

  • the device’s advertising ID and similar technical identifiers;
  • the IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
  • device and system information (model, Android version, language);
  • ad interaction data (impression, click, video completion).

Google uses this data to serve, measure and, depending on your consent, personalize ads. Before showing the first ad, the app asks for your consent through Google’s official platform (User Messaging Platform, compliant with the European IAB TCF framework) for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland; you can choose non-personalized ads there. To learn more about how Google processes this data, see Google’s privacy policy and its page on advertising technologies.

3a. Google Play Games services (optional)

Since version 2.0, you can choose, on the “Profile” screen, to use Google Play Games to compare your records on leaderboards and earn Play Games achievements. It is never required: without this choice, the app does not connect to Play Games, shows no sign-in window and sends nothing to this service; the game works exactly the same, offline included.

If you turn it on, sign-in goes through your Google account and the service is provided by Google (a data controller separate from the developer). The following is then sent to Google: your Play Games player identity (gamer name, avatar), your best Classic scores in each game and in the Arcade Tour, and the achievements you unlock. No other app data is sent, and the app stores no saved game with Google. Google’s SDK may also collect diagnostic and performance data. Your profile visibility (friends, everyone, only you) is set in the Play Games app.

To stop: “Stop using Play Games” on the profile screen (nothing more is sent). To unlink your account or delete data already sent: your Play Games profile or your Google account. The developer has no access to this data beyond the game’s public leaderboards.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your scores, statistics, progress and settings stay on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data from Android settings. If Android backup is turned on on your device, this progress data may be included in your Google account backup (managed by Android, encrypted, not accessible to the developer) and restored if you reinstall the app or change phones; you can turn this backup off in Android settings. Ad and consent data are excluded from this backup. Data processed by Google AdMob is kept according to Google’s own rules, described in its privacy policy (link above).

7. Intended audience

This app is a general-audience game, with no violent or shocking content, suitable for all ages. By design, the developer collects no data from any user. Ads are subject to the consent process described in section 3 before any personalization. You can change or withdraw this consent at any time from the “Privacy and ads” link in the app’s main menu.

8. Changes to this policy

If the way the app works changes in a way that affects what is described above, this page will be updated accordingly and the date at the top of the page will be refreshed.
